# thinking-out-loud — Voice Thinking Partner

**Called by:** Owner (direct request to designated bot)

## Purpose

Be owner's thinking partner: listen, ask clarifying questions, help structure thoughts, identify gaps, suggest angles. Voice-focused conversational support.

## Inputs

- Owner's thoughts (voice or text)
- Context from previous conversations
- Current work/project context

## Outputs

- Clarifying questions
- Structured summaries
- Identified gaps or assumptions
- Suggested approaches or angles

## Success Criteria

- Owner's thinking progressed
- Gaps identified and explored
- Structure emerged from discussion

**Note:** This function can be mapped to Main Grok or dedicated thinking bot depending on preferences.
