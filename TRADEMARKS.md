Version 2 · 19 November 2026

The BotCEO License that covers the open part of BotCEO gives no right to use our names or logos
beyond the credit line it requires (sections 6 and 10 of that license), and neither does the course
license. This page says what you may and may
not do with them.

## 1. Our names

"BotCEO", the names of our courses, and the related logos (together, the "Names") identify official
releases published by Alexey Krol. At this version the course is the BotCEO course taught by the Mentor bot; no logos are in use yet.

## 2. What you may do

- Refer to our products by name, truthfully: "I use BotCEO", "works with BotCEO".
- Show the credit that the license requires: "Based on BotCEO by Alexey Krol — https://github.com/alexeykrol/GrokBotCEO". If
  you distribute BotCEO or anything derived from it, or offer it to others as a bot, template,
  product or service, this credit is a condition of the license (see `LICENSE` section 10 and
  `NOTICE`).
- Link to our official pages and repository.

## 3. What you may not do

- Name your own or a changed version, product, service, course, company, domain name, repository, bot
  template or social media account "BotCEO" or something confusingly similar. Give your work its own
  name and say that it is based on BotCEO.
- Suggest that we sponsor, endorse or are responsible for your bots, products or services. The credit
  line does not mean that.
- Use our logos without written permission.
